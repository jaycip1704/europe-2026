Instagram follower tracker

Follow Ledger

See who doesn’t follow you back and who unfollowed you, using the data export Instagram gives you. No login and no password. Your files are read in this browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Drop your Instagram export here The whole .zip, or followers_1.json and following.json together. HTML exports work too.
How to get your export from Instagram
  1. In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap the menu, then Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export.
  3. Pick your Instagram profile, then Export to device.
  4. Under Customize information, select only Followers and following. Set date range to All time and format to JSON.
  5. Start the export. Instagram emails you when it’s ready, usually within minutes.
  6. Download the zip and drop it above. Repeat every week or two: each new export is compared with the last one to find who unfollowed you.

The export doesn’t include other accounts’ follower counts. Type them into the box on each row (like 850, 12k or 2.4m) to use the follower filter. Counts, Big tags and hidden accounts are remembered across exports.
